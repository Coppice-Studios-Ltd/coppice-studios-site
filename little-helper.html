Our products / Little Helper

The one app for life with a toddler.

Little Helper puts the tools parents reach for every day into one calm, simple app. A locked photo gallery, a rise and shine clock, a noise machine, finger painting, alphabet tracing and illustrated stories. No account, no adverts, and nothing leaves your phone.

Why we built it

We built the app we wanted at 5am.

As new parents, we found a lot of things overwhelming and expensive. A wake-up clock for the bedroom. A white noise machine. Hiding the camera roll from small fingers. Apps playing adverts next to a sleeping toddler.

Little Helper is a small toolkit, not a big platform. A few things done simply, so you're not juggling lots of different apps at bedtime.

What's inside

Six tools. One calm home screen.

Each tile solves a moment most parents know well.

Waiting rooms and long journeys

Photo Playpen

Pick the photos, then hand over the phone. Your toddler can swipe freely but can't delete, share, call anyone or leave the screen.

The 5am wake-up

Rise & Shine Clock

A sleepy moon means stay in bed. A bright sun means it's time to get up. Set a wake-up time once and the sun appears on its own. An affordable way to try a bedtime clock before buying one.

Naps and bedtime

Noise Machine

Sleepy sounds in one tap, with a dimmable dark screen that doubles as a nightlight. Set how long it plays. No ads, ever.

Rainy afternoons

Finger Paint

Colours and marks with just a finger. A locked area to make a mess, without the mess.

First letters

Alphabet Painting

Trace each letter with a finger, then see what it stands for. b is for banana. One letter at a time, a to z.

Bedtime stories

Story Time

An expanding collection of illustrated children's stories, sorted by read length and age. Created by people, never AI.

Peace of mind

Set up by grown-ups. Locked for little ones.

Grown-ups-only exit

To leave a locked screen, an adult types the numbers shown. Easy for you, a puzzle for a toddler.

Only the tiles you want

Turn any tool on or off in Settings and it disappears from your child's home screen. Adjust wake-up times and noise duration in the same place.

Your photos stay yours

You choose exactly which photos appear in the gallery. Nothing is uploaded and we never see them.

No account, no adverts

Nothing to sign into. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.
